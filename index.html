How to interpret the data

Each card's date is the primary publication or evidence date, except cards explicitly referencing the November 12 review of an undated supplier page. All numbers are attributed to their originating sources. For Klarna, the 80% figure refers specifically to chats during calendar 2025; it is not customer satisfaction or jobs eliminated. For Kuken, 90% annual savings is a projection. The Air Canada chatbot architecture was not established in the tribunal decision.

To keep this resource continually current, a future hosted version would need scheduled source ingestion, record deduplication, link checks and evidence-gated publication. This edition loads an approved structured JSON dataset. Scheduled discovery is not yet enabled; only reviewed records are published.
